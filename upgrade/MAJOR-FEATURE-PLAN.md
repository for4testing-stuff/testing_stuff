# BOURSE·DAILY — Major Feature Expansion Plan

## Feature 1: Advanced Portfolio Risk & Performance Analytics Dashboard
- **What it is**: A comprehensive analytics suite integrated into `watchlist.html` (Portfolio section).
- **Capabilities**:
  - Asset Allocation percentage breakdown by sector with visual progress bars.
  - Value at Risk (VaR 95%) and Portfolio Beta calculation based on constituent volatilities.
  - Transaction History Log (Buy/Sell trade history stored in `localStorage`).
  - Estimated Annual Dividend Income calculation.

## Feature 2: Side-by-Side Multi-Company Comparison Tool (`compare.html`)
- **What it is**: A dedicated comparison page allowing users to select 2 to 4 companies for deep side-by-side analysis.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Side-by-side fundamental comparison table (Price, M-Cap, P/E, 52W High/Low, Sector).
  - Normalized canvas comparison chart overlaying price trajectories of compared stocks.
  - Quick-add symbol selector with autocomplete and instant comparison updates.
