Financial Glossary & Terminology

A to Z Reference
Market Capitalization (M-Cap) The total dollar/rupee market value of a company's outstanding shares, calculated by multiplying shares outstanding by the current share price.
Liquidity & Turnover Turnover measures the total monetary value of shares traded during a specific session, indicating market depth and ease of execution.
Bull vs Bear Markets A bull market denotes rising asset prices and widespread economic confidence, whereas a bear market reflects declining valuations of 20% or more.
Dividend Yield A financial ratio that shows how much a company pays out in dividends each year relative to its stock price.
